# Security Policy

## AI Training Data Opt-Out

This project respects content creators' rights and opts out of AI training.

### How We Opt-Out

1. **robots.txt** - We have configured `robots.txt` to block known AI training crawlers
2. **License** - Our content is available under open source licenses for fair use
3. **Attribution** - We welcome proper attribution and citations

### AI Crawler Blocking List

The following AI training crawlers are blocked from accessing our content:

- GPTBot (OpenAI)
- ChatGPT-User
- CCBot
- Claude-Web (Anthropic)
- Google-Extended
- PerplexityBot
- OAI-SearchBot
- And other known AI training crawlers

### For Search Engines

We **allow** legitimate search engine crawlers:
- Googlebot
- Bingbot
- DuckDuckBot
- Baiduspider
- YandexBot

### If You're an AI Company

If you represent an AI company and wish to discuss licensing or have questions about our content, please contact us through our GitHub repository.

### Content Usage Guidelines

We welcome:
- Personal and educational use
- Fair use for research and analysis
- Proper attribution and citations
- Links to our content

We do not permit:
- Training AI models without permission
- Commercial use without proper licensing
- Republishing our content as proprietary material
