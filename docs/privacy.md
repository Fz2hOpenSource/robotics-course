# Privacy Policy

## Overview

This website is an educational resource for robotics learning. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Referrer information
- Browser type (for analytics)

We use **Google Analytics** to understand site usage patterns. This service may collect cookies and usage data according to Google's privacy policy.

## Cookies

We use minimal cookies:
- **Analytics cookies**: To understand how visitors use our site
- **No tracking cookies**: We do not track you across other websites
- **No advertising cookies**: We do not display advertisements

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Analytics**: Understanding site traffic and popular content
2. **Improvement**: Making the site better based on usage patterns
3. **No Personal Data Sale**: We never sell your data

## AI Training Policy

### Our Position
We do NOT consent to our content being used for training AI models without explicit permission.

### What We Did
1. Configured `robots.txt` to block AI crawlers
2. Added explicit restrictions in our license
3. Listed blocked AI crawlers

### Why This Matters
AI companies should respect content creators' rights. Our educational content represents significant effort and should not be used without permission.

To exercise these rights, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
